HEAD CHEF

Maxsuel Xistoli

🇧🇷 BORN IN ESPIRITO SANTO, BRAZIL 🇧🇷

Maxsuel brings the true flavours of the streets to Twickenham.

With years of experience in Brazilian cuisine, he's passionate about grilling the finest ingredients and sharing the vibrant culture, warmth and spirit of Brazil in every dish.

Every meal is made with love, inspired by tradition and made to bring people together.

🇧🇷

FOUNDERS

Darren Muylders
& Andrea Pancione

Founded by Darren Muylders and Andrea Pancione, Sabor de Rua Brasil was created from a shared vision: to bring an authentic Brazilian dining experience to Twickenham while creating a place where the Brazilian community and food lovers can come together.

With Darren's experience at the prestigious Savoy Hotel and Andrea's background with Gaucho restaurants, the founders bring a wealth of hospitality and restaurant expertise, with a commitment to delivering a five-star experience in the heart of Twickenham.

Together with Head Chef Maxsuel Xistoli, born in Espírito Santo, Brazil, the team invites you to discover an unforgettable dining experience at The Three Kings.
